Every night, a bank has to close its books. I own that job at Capital One — the run that posts the day's transactions, settles balances and cuts the ledger across Card and Bank. It gets one shot, so most of my work is making a very large batch fail loudly and early instead of quietly at the last minute.

Off the clock I build small tools that use an AI model but keep everything on your own laptop — a daily briefing, a receipt scanner. The fun part isn't the model, it's making something that guesses behave like something that doesn't.
