Background

Two degrees from NC State — computer science and applied maths, 4.0, on a Park Scholarship: a full ride given to roughly the top 1% of applicants for leadership and service rather than grades.

And eight years with InspireNC, a STEAM non-profit in the Triangle. I joined at seventeen as a programmer on FRC Team 6908, “Infuzed”, and left five years later as its Chairman — free bootcamps, Java to cohorts of 35, a competitive robotics season to keep funded and building. 100+ students through it.

I have been teaching people to program for almost as long as I have been programming.

› the longer version

I was a mechanical designer before I was a programmer on that team — building the robot, then writing what drove it. Robotics Representative, then Chairman for five years, most of it spent getting other people started rather than competing myself.

Alongside it I was a teaching assistant for Advanced MATLAB in NC State's CS department: over 1,000 questions answered on the course forum, and an assignment set rebuilt around where students were actually getting stuck rather than where the syllabus assumed they would.

It shows up in how I build. The write-ups on this site are the same instinct — explain the decision, name the thing that nearly went wrong, say what I would do differently. A system nobody else can reason about has one point of failure, and it is usually me.
